## Kothalipi 0.1.2

- **Bangla and English together.** Say Bangla with English words in the middle ("আমি meeting এ যাব") and each word is written in its own script. The Bangla key now sends both languages to Gemini and OpenAI. The English key stays English only.
- **Keep talking while it types.** In Cloud mode the next recording can start the moment you let go, so nothing you say is lost while the last one is on its way. Text is always typed in the order you spoke it.
- **Faster uploads.** The connection to Gemini or OpenAI opens while you are still speaking, audio is sent as FLAC (about half the size of WAV), and one connection is reused between dictations.
- **Accuracy check.** `scripts/eval` measures word and character error rates for English, Bangla and mixed sentences, so a change that hurts accuracy is caught.
