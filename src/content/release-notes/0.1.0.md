# Welcome to Kothalipi

Voice typing in Bangla and English, in any app.

- Hold **Right Command** (Windows: **Right Alt**) to speak Bangla, **fn** (Windows: **Right Ctrl**) to speak English.
- Choose **Offline** (free, private) or **Cloud** with your own Gemini or OpenAI key. If a key runs out or you go offline, Kothalipi falls back automatically.
- Setup picks the best voice models for your computer.
