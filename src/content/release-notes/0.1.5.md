## Kothalipi 0.1.5

- **Bangla to English Voice Translation.** Speak in Bangla, and have it naturally translated into fluent English using Google Gemini in Post-Processing.
- **Built-in Google Gemini Provider.** Direct support for Google Gemini in Post-Processing with automatic API key sharing from General settings.
- **Huge Performance & Bundle Optimizations.** Initial app load JavaScript payload reduced by over 67% (saved >1.2 MB) through on-demand language loading and lazy-loaded settings components.
- **Signed Auto-Updates.** Configured signed update artifacts and updater endpoints for automatic update notifications and 1-click update installations.
