## Kothalipi 0.1.4

- **Smart Polish (new, off by default).** After typing, Gemini fixes Bangla spelling, adds । ? and commas, drops filler sounds, and keeps English words in English letters. Turn it on in Settings, General, Listening. It needs a Gemini key, sends only the text (never audio), and adds about a second. If Gemini is slow or unreachable, your original text is typed. The raw text stays in History.
- **Safe by design.** What you say is treated as text, never as instructions, and an answer that looks like a rewrite or a reply (much longer or shorter, or chatty) is thrown away.
